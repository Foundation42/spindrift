# Spindrift, closed — the keep holds, and G7 is the next gate

*CC, 2026-09-02, the close-out of the campaign brief (`docs/cc-brief-spindrift.md`)
and its plan (`docs/spindrift-campaign.md`). Six beats, one half-beat, and
the closing beat P5 (`docs/cc-report-beat5.md`). Written to be read by
someone who has read none of the beat reports.*

## What was built, in one paragraph

A particle system that is a rill program whose plane is the row. The
population is a struct-of-arrays store of Q16.16 integers with (id, gen)
handles and a freelist, summarised on the plane as `drift/@name/{count,
bounds, digest, refused, coarsened, throttled, mounted}`; a spray ticks in
six phases — broadcasts, materialise, spawn, sweep (chunked over the
shared JobSystem), reap, cast — on fed time only; the words are `spawn`,
`gravity`, `perish`, `hear`, `over`, and the host's `collide`, `ground`,
`stick`; fields flow both ways through the engine's own field store; the
engine renders rows as sprites and as capped point lights through a
dynamic-tree leaf, collides them against its static tree through the CPU
twin tracer, and schedules sprays under a row-steps budget with a
governor that writes the knob on the transcript. Every beat closed with
its gate green and its mutations bitten, and every ruling is in §7 of
the campaign — twenty-eight, with beat 5's six asked in its report.

## G7, restated as the next campaign's opening gate

**G7 — two evaluators, one text.** The same kernel `.rill`, evaluated on
CPU rows and on the GPU, produces the same population after N ticks —
bit-identical, not tolerance-close. What this campaign paid for it:

- **Integer rows.** Every sim number is Q16.16 (`fixed.zig`,
  `rill.row.Val`); `+ − × ÷` and the words built on them are exact on
  both evaluators by construction, and the transcendental words are not
  in the row plane until they earn an integer kernel (recon R-a §5, ruling
  3). `over` is a lerp; `hear` is trilinear over integers; `collide`
  answers in fixed point.
- **The row plane.** A kernel is a rill program whose plane is the row
  (rill spec §3.16): the column, the row runtime, the snapshot-and-write
  rule, folded arrays, the pipe's carry-by-name — one text, one graph,
  ready for a second evaluator to walk.
- **The float boundary, crossed once per tick.** Broadcasts, lattices,
  the tracer's answer and the cast are converted at the host, once, per
  tick, never per row; a GPU evaluator inherits the same boundary.
- **Replay on the transcript.** Dumps AND what the sim says are
  byte-identical across runs; a wall-clock governor writes a knob that
  rides the log; the population dump (format 3) is the bit-identity
  witness G7 compares.

The trigger stands as ruled: the first customer scene where a kernel
misses its budget on the 9950X3D.
